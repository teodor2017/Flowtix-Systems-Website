The Problem

Urban traffic is broken —
and the cost is real

Cities across Romania and beyond are running on static traffic signal timings designed decades ago. Meanwhile, urban populations have surged, vehicle counts have exploded, and travel times have tripled on some corridors during peak hours.

A journey that should take 10 minutes now takes 30. A 30-minute drive stretches to over an hour. Millions of commuters experience this daily — sometimes more than once.

"Intersections are the neuralgic point of the road infrastructure."

— Carolin Treichl, EVP EMENA, Kapsch TrafficCom

Architecture

A two-level intelligence engine

Level 1

Local Intersection Optimization

Detects real-time traffic flow around each intersection and adaptively adjusts phase durations for all incoming streets — reducing queue lengths and per-vehicle wait times.

01
Level 2

Zone-Level Network Analysis

Analyses traffic flows across corridors, segments and entire zones — not just at individual intersections. Estimates average speeds, identifies high-density corridors, and coordinates signals across multiple junctions simultaneously to balance macro traffic flows.

02
Unique Coverage

Pedestrian Crossings & Secondary Streets

Unlike most existing systems that focus exclusively on major junctions, Flowtix integrates pedestrian crossings and secondary streets directly into the optimization model — significantly reducing frequent interruptions to traffic flow on main arterial roads.
